# Unit guide portraits

All PNG files are original game artwork copied without modification. The representative portrait identifies a unit or the base member of a grouped line; it does not represent every upgrade, skin or civilization variant.

- Age of Empires II: [AoE II Companion](https://github.com/denniske/aoe2companion), revision `797538c97f3cd957bf700b23b90cdaf808e4fa80`, `assets/units/`.
- Age of Empires IV: [AoE4 World data](https://github.com/aoe4world/data), revision `e89f01e598998bf76bdddfbf2afce1659fee00cf`, `images/units/`.
- StarCraft II: [SC2 Planner](https://github.com/BurnySc2/sc2-planner), revision `96fb45afef85c558b84cd1753dac5f2ece57dc0a`, `src/icons/png/`.

MatchDeck is an unofficial community project, not endorsed by Microsoft, Blizzard or the source projects.

Run `node scripts/sync-guide-artwork.mjs` to reproduce the files. `provenance.json` records source paths, pinned revisions and SHA-256 checksums. Images are served locally; visitors do not load them from external providers.
